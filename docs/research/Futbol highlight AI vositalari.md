# Football highlight Shorts'ni texnik yasash oson, pul topish qiyin

**Qisqa javob:** individual creator to'liq football match'ni olib, gol, xavfli moment va yaxshi hujumlarni o'zi topadigan, istalgan uzunlikda clip kesadigan, action'ga ergashib 9:16 vertical'ga reframe qiladigan va score'ni hisobga olgan "GOAL"/jamoa nomi/hisob overlay'larini qo'shadigan **tayyor vositani sotib ololmaydi**. Bunday "hammasi birda" tizimlar bor (WSC Sports, Magnifi, Pixellot), lekin ular faqat league va broadcaster'larga demo va individual narx orqali sotiladi. Creator'lar uchun arzon vositalar (OpusClip $0–29/oy, Klap $14/oydan) 9:16 export qiladi, ammo ular moment'ni asosan ovoz va commentator hayajoniga qarab topadi, football'ni "tushunmaydi". **Texnik jihatdan esa buni o'zingiz qura olasiz.** RTX 5090 va Windows bunga yetarli: scoreboard OCR, audio peak, ixtiyoriy Gemini va ball tracking asosidagi pipeline bir match'ni taxminan 10–30 daqiqada qayta ishlaydi. Asosiy to'siq texnologiya emas, balki **huquq va pul**. Uzbekistan hozircha YouTube Partner Program (YPP) mamlakatlari ro'yxatida yo'q, demak YouTube'dan reklama daromadi hozir umuman olinmaydi. Bundan tashqari, Premier League, UCL yoki LaLiga broadcast footage'idan qilingan Short Content ID'ga mos kelsa, rights owner qanday policy tanlaganidan qat'i nazar bloklanadi, qo'lda yuborilgan takedown esa strike keltiradi. Shuning uchun bu vositani o'z footage'ingizga (havaskor yoki mahalliy match'lar, ruxsat olingan kontent) qo'llash yoki xizmat sifatida sotish mantiqli. TV match'larni kesib monetize qilish rejasi ishlamaydi.

## Football'ni tushunadigan vositalar faqat enterprise'ga sotiladi

Football event'larini haqiqatan aniqlaydigan platformalar yirik mijozlar uchun qurilgan. **WSC Sports** NBA, LaLiga, DAZN va YouTube kabi mijozlarga xizmat qiladi. Saytida faqat "Request a demo" tugmasi bor, narx yoki creator plan yo'q ([WSC Sports](https://wsc-sports.com/)). 2025-yilda u **16 million highlight** yaratgan va **124 million+ sport event**'ini aniqlagan ([i24news](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/fifa-world-cup-2026/artc-the-first-ai-world-cup-the-revolution-taking-place-in-the-tournament)). **Magnifi** (VideoVerse, 2025-yildan Minute Media'ga tegishli) computer vision (kompyuterga tasvirni "ko'rish"ni o'rgatuvchi AI sohasi), NLP va audio signallar yordamida highlight kesadi, lekin narxi "price on request" ([SVG](https://www.sportsvideo.org/?p=290895); [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/magnifi)). **Pixellot** gol, zarba, corner va foul'larni aniqlaydi, ammo faqat institutional mijozlar bilan ishlaydi ([totalmedia.ai](https://www.totalmedia.ai/en/resources/blog/best-ai-sports-highlight-generator-apps-2026)).

Oddiy odam sotib olishi mumkin bo'lgan **Veo, Trace, XbotGo, Hudl** esa boshqa vazifa uchun. Ular faqat o'z kamerasidan yoki jamoa tahlili uchun yuklangan footage'dan highlight yasaydi. Veo kamerasi taxminan $1,300–1,900 turadi, ustiga subscription bor. Trace yiliga $180 dan boshlanadi. XbotGo'da avtomatik gol aniqlash yo'q va watermark'ni olib tashlab bo'lmaydi ([sportssteps.com](https://sportssteps.com/pixellot-vs-veo-vs-xbotgo-best-ai-cameras-youth-sports/); [Trace](https://traceup.com/academy/best-ai-sports-cameras-for-automatically-recording-youth-games-in-2026)). Bularning birortasi TV broadcast faylini olib Shorts kesish uchun mo'ljallanmagan. Ularning birortasida 9:16 output borligi manbalarda tasdiqlanmagan.

Generic AI clipper'lar arzon va 9:16 chiqaradi, lekin football'ga yarim-yorti mos keladi. **OpusClip** narxlari: Free $0 (watermark bilan), Starter $15, Pro $29. Free va Starter'da 10GB gacha, Pro'da 30GB gacha upload mumkin ([OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)). Mustaqil sharhga ko'ra, uning sport footage'dagi moment detection'i talking-head videolarga qaraganda "noticeably weaker", ya'ni clip'larni qo'lda to'g'rilashga to'g'ri keladi ([toolsforhumans.ai](https://www.toolsforhumans.ai/ai-tools/opus-clip)). Boshqa sharhlovchi soccer match yuklab, "all goals" deb prompt bergan. Natija umuman yaxshi bo'lgan, lekin ba'zi clip'lar keskin uzilib qolgan ([shotkit.com](https://shotkit.com/opus-clip-review-ai-viral-shorts/)). **Klap** generic vositalar orasida eng ko'p narsani va'da qiladi: 4 soatgacha video, speech'siz (harakat intensivligi va audio energiyasi orqali) gol topish, "AI Reframe 2" bilan action'ga ergashish, team ranglari uchun Brand Kit. Barcha plan'larda watermark yo'q ([Klap](https://klap.app/tools/sports-highlight-video-maker)). Bu da'volar mustaqil tekshirilmagan. Pippit, Short.ai va Vidio'ning "AI football highlights generator" sahifalari asosan bir xil generic engine ustidagi marketing sahifalariga o'xshaydi ([Pippit](https://www.pippit.ai/blog/world-cup/ai-soccer-highlight-generator); [Short.ai](https://www.short.ai/ai-clip-maker/football-highlight-video)).

## O'zingiz qurishingiz mumkin bo'lgan pipeline sakkiz qadamdan iborat

To'liq "broadcast → vertical Shorts + overlay" qiladigan yetuk open-source loyiha topilmadi. Lekin har bir qism uchun ishlaydigan, license jihatdan toza komponent bor. Eng ishonchli gol detektori AI model emas, balki **scoreboard OCR** (Optical Character Recognition, rasmdagi matnni o'qish). Ekran burchagidagi score-bug sekundiga bir marta o'qiladi va hisob o'zgarganda gol bo'lgani aniq bo'ladi. Haqiqiy gol grafik yangilanishidan taxminan 10–40 sekund oldin sodir bo'ladi. Laravel tilida aytsak, bu model "taxmin qilishi" emas, ma'lumotlar bazasidagi `score` ustuni o'zgarganini kuzatishning o'zi. Bu yondashuv ham patent, ham vendor arxitekturalarida ishlatiladi ([USPTO 10970554](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/10970554); [Mixpeek](https://blog.mixpeek.com/ai-video-analysis-sports/)). Faqat tasvirga qarab gol topish esa qiyin: Stanford CS231n loyihasida 25 match'da atigi **0.56 recall** olingan, ya'ni gollarning taxminan yarmi topilmagan ([CS231n 2024](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/2024/papers/automatic-soccer-game-highlight-detection.pdf)).

Gol bo'lmagan xavfli moment'larni (zarba, sejv, kuchli hujum) **audio signal** ushlaydi: tomosha hayqirig'i, commentator ovozining balandligi va tezligi. Audio event score'larini commentary transkripti bilan birlashtirish faqat bitta signalga tayanishdan "significantly" yaxshi natija bergan ([UCP paper](https://repositorio.ucp.pt/bitstreams/b5093cc6-62de-496b-b198-14398f72677f/download)). Whisper (OpenAI'ning nutqni matnga aylantiruvchi modeli) "GOAL!" kabi kalit so'zlarni topib beradi va subtitle'larni ham tekinga chiqaradi.

Aniqroq event topish uchun **action spotting** modellari bor. Ular videodagi "gol", "zarba", "corner" lahzasini sekundlar aniqligida belgilaydi. Hozirgi reference model **T-DEED**: u 2024-yilgi SoccerNet challenge'ida g'olib bo'lgan, 2025-yil g'olibi esa uning variantida **60.03 Team-mAP@1** natija olgan ([arXiv 2508.19182](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.19182v1); [T-DEED GitHub](https://github.com/arturxe2/T-DEED)). Muammo shundaki, pretrained weights (oldindan o'qitilgan model fayllari) SoccerNet videolarida o'qitilgan. Bu videolarga faqat NDA imzolab kirish mumkin, hosila datasetlar esa non-commercial ([soccer-net.org](https://www.soccer-net.org/data); [SoccerChat](https://datasets.simula.no/soccerchat/)). Monetize qilinadigan kanal uchun bu weights'ni xavfli deb hisoblang.

Arzonroq muqobil **Gemini**. U butun 90 daqiqalik match'ni bitta so'rovda qabul qiladi: 1M-context modellarda low resolution'da 3 soatgacha video. Tokenlar soni: video uchun sekundiga ~100, audio uchun ~32 ([Gemini docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/video-understanding)). Narxlardan hisoblaganda, bir match taxminan 713k token, ya'ni 3.8-flash'da **~$0.53**, 3.5-flash-lite'da **~$0.21** chiqadi. Batch API bilan bu yana ikki baravar arzon. 2027-01-01 dan narxlar ko'tariladi ([Gemini pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)). Kamchiligi: standart sampling 1 fps, shuning uchun tez action detallari yo'qoladi. Gemini bergan vaqtni ±10 sekundlik "oyna" deb qabul qiling va aniq chegarani lokal signal bilan aniqlang.

Vertical reframe uchun to'p va o'yinchilarni topish kerak. Bu yerda license muhim. **Ultralytics YOLO'dan foydalanmang:** Ultralytics o'qitilgan modellar ham, ichki foydalanish ham AGPL ostida ekanini aytadi, yopiq tijoriy foydalanish uchun esa pullik Enterprise license kerak ([Ultralytics license](https://www.ultralytics.com/license)). Uning o'rniga **RF-DETR** (Apache 2.0, tijoriy foydalanish bepul) ([Roboflow](https://blog.roboflow.com/rf-detr-is-free-to-use-commercially/)) hamda **roboflow/sports** va `supervision` (MIT) bilan ByteTrack/BoT-SORT tracking'dan foydalaning ([roboflow/sports](https://github.com/roboflow/sports)). Roboflow'ning tutorial'ida RF-DETR-small player, ball va goal uchun **mAP50 90.1** natija bergan. Lekin bu bolalar football'i ustidagi prototip, 60 sekundlik clip M-series Mac'da ~15 daqiqa ishlangan, va 2D tasvirdan gol bilan sejvni ajratib bo'lmagan ([Roboflow tutorial](https://roboflow.com/blog/make-automatic-highlight-reels-from-soccer-games)). To'p kichik, xira va tez-tez to'silib qoladi. Shuning uchun crop markazini to'p va uning atrofidagi o'yinchilar o'rtachasidan hisoblash, to'p yo'qolganda o'yinchilar markaziga qaytish va natijani silliqlash (EMA yoki one-euro filter) kerak. Google'ning MediaPipe AutoFlip vositasi 2023-03-01 dan beri qo'llab-quvvatlanmaydi ([MediaPipe](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/guide)), shuning uchun crop'ni o'zingiz yozasiz.

Pipeline tartibi quyidagicha. (1) FFmpeg NVDEC bilan videoni o'qiydi. (2) Audio peak'lar, Whisper kalit so'zlari va scoreboard OCR (PaddleOCR, Apache 2.0) "nomzod" moment'larni beradi. (3) Ixtiyoriy ravishda Gemini Flash-Lite har bir oynaga "gol/zarba/sejv" label'i va caption yozadi. (4) Foydalanuvchi tanlagan uzunlikda clip kesiladi, masalan event'dan 8 sekund oldin va 4 sekund keyin, kerak bo'lsa replay bilan. (5) RF-DETR va ByteTrack bilan 9:16 crop qilinadi. (6) FFmpeg `drawtext`/`overlay` bilan "GOAL" animatsiyasi, jamoa nomlari va OCR'dan olingan real hisob qo'shiladi. (7) Natija `h264_nvenc` bilan 1080×1920 formatga encode qilinadi. Detection'ni butun 90 daqiqaga emas, faqat nomzod clip'larga ishlatsangiz, RTX 5090'da bir match **taxminan 10–30 daqiqa** oladi. Bu benchmark emas, hisob-kitob asosidagi taxmin.

Windows uchun bitta ogohlantirish: 5090 (sm_120) CUDA 12.8+ ostida build qilingan PyTorch talab qiladi. Sizning cu130 venv'ingiz mos keladi. Lekin torch versiyasini 2.7 dan past qilib pin qilgan eski SoccerNet repo'lari "no kernel image" xatosi beradi ([PyTorch forums](https://discuss.pytorch.org/t/nvidia-geforce-rtx-5090-with-cuda-capability-sm-120-is-not-compatible-with-the-current-pytorch-installation/216518)). decord va mmcv o'rniga PyAV yoki OpenCV ishlating.

## Uzbekistan'dan YouTube reklama daromadi hozircha yopiq

Bu eng muhim moliyaviy fakt. YouTube'ning rasmiy YPP mamlakatlari ro'yxatida **Uzbekistan yo'q** ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en)). 2026-09-23 dagi xabarga ko'ra, Saida Mirziyoyeva Google VP Karan Bhatia bilan monetizatsiyani yoqish masalasini muhokama qilgan, ammo **ishga tushirish sanasi belgilanmagan**. Qo'shni Kazakhstan esa ro'yxatda bor ([Kursiv Media](https://uz.kursiv.media/en/2026-09-23/youtube-monetisation-uzbekistan-google/)). YPP'ga kirish uchun 1,000 subscriber hamda yo 12 oyda 4,000 soat, yo 90 kunda 10M Shorts ko'rish kerak. Eng asosiysi, siz YPP mamlakatida **yashashingiz** shart ([YouTube Help: YPP](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)). Kanal mamlakatini boshqa davlatga o'zgartirish shartlarni buzish hisoblanadi va daromadni yo'qotish xavfini tug'diradi.

YPP ochilganda ham Shorts daromadi kichik. Shorts RPM (1,000 ko'rish uchun creator oladigan pul) bo'yicha uchinchi tomon hisoblari AQSh uchun ~**$0.33**, Avstraliya uchun ~$0.19 ([AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/monetization/what-rpm-can-you-expect-from-shorts-in-2026)), niche bo'yicha esa 5–50¢ ([Satura AI](https://saturaai.com/blog/youtube-shorts-rpm-explained-niche-earnings)). Creator Shorts pool'idan 45% oladi ([Shorts monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)). O'zbek yoki MDH auditoriyasining RPM'i past tomonda bo'lishi kutiladi. Bu manbasiz taxmin.

## Broadcast footage Shorts'da bloklanadi va kanalni yo'qotish xavfi bor

Gap shundaki, oddiy videolarda Content ID claim ko'pincha "claim qilindi, lekin pulni rights owner oladi" degan natija beradi. **Shorts'da esa bunday emas:** 1–3 daqiqalik vertical video'da faol claim bo'lsa, rights owner tanlagan policy'dan qat'i nazar video **bloklanadi** ([YouTube Help: Shorts rights](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13053317?hl=en)). Claim qilingan kontentli, bir daqiqadan uzun Shorts monetize qilinmaydi ([Shorts monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)). Claim strike emas. Lekin rights owner qo'lda takedown yuborsa, strike tushadi va **90 kun ichida 3 strike kanalni o'chiradi**, shu kanalga bog'langan kanallar bilan birga ([Copyright strike basics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2814000?hl=en)). Bitta rights owner bir paytda bir nechta notice yuborsa, ko'p clip joylagan kanal bir necha kunda yopilishi mumkin.

League'lar buni jiddiy nazorat qiladi. Premier League London va Singapore'da yuristlar va tergovchilar jamoasini saqlaydi, 2023/24 mavsumida ~650,000 stream'ni bloklagan ([Calcio e Finanza](https://www.calcioefinanza.it/2025/02/07/premier-league-contro-pirateria-23-24/)). 2014-yildan beri Vine'dagi gol clip'lariga qarshi kurashib keladi ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/internet/premier-league-disallows-goals-uploaded-to-vine-on-eve-of-new-season-1261703)). 2023-yilda hatto Sidemen Charity Match VOD'i ham vaqtincha olib tashlangan ([GGRecon](https://ggrecon.com/articles/sidemen-charity-match-pulled-from-youtube-by-the-premier-league)). Boshqa sportdan misol: CFL league o'zining YouTube highlight'larini ishga tushirgan paytda fan kanali "CFL Mixtapes" uchta video uchun yopilgan ([Techdirt](https://www.techdirt.com/tag/adam-stevens/)).

"GOAL" yozuvi va jamoa nomlari qo'shish bu muammoni hal qilmaydi. YouTube reused content uchun **meaningful transformation**ni talab qiladi: commentary, jiddiy montaj yoki kamerada o'zingizning ishtirokingiz. U "unedited clips from TV shows" va "compilations with no original content added"ni aniq taqiqlaydi ([Channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392); [Shorts policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)). 2025-07-15 dan "inauthentic content" policy'si mass-produced va shablonli kontentni ham qamraydi ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/)). AI bilan avtomatik ko'paytirilgan bir xil clip'lar aynan shu ta'rifga tushadi. Fair use AQSh huquqidagi himoya va Content ID'ning avtomatik match qilishini to'xtatmaydi. Instagram ham bundan yaxshi emas. Meta 2025-07-14 dan unoriginal repost qiluvchi akkauntlarning reach'ini kesadi va monetizatsiyasini o'chiradi ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/facebook-introduces-stronger-measures-against-unoriginal-content-creators/)). Rights Manager esa fingerprint (videoning raqamli "barmoq izi") orqali nusxalarni topadi ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/media/metas-new-tool-gives-reels-creators-power-over-stolen-clips-1.500350782)).

## Qonuniy yo'llar: o'z footage'ingiz, ruxsat va o'yin kontenti

Pipeline o'zi sizga tegishli yoki ruxsat olingan footage bilan ishlaganda eng katta qiymat beradi. **Havaskor, yoshlar va mahalliy match'lar**ni o'zingiz suratga olsangiz yoki tashkilotchi/klubdan yozma ruxsat olsangiz (voyaga yetmaganlar uchun ota-onadan ham), Content ID xavfi yo'q va bitta match'dan o'nlab Shorts chiqadi. Bu natija hisoblangan xulosa, manbada tekshirilmagan. Uzbekistan Super League'ni UzPFL boshqaradi ([pfl.uz](https://pfl.uz); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uzbekistan_Super_League)). 2026-02-25 dagi yagona manbaga ko'ra, Yandex Uzbekistan Super va Pro League match'larini Allplay va Kinopoisk'da ko'rsatish huquqini olgan ([Zamin.uz](https://zamin.uz/en/sport/191531-yandex-uzbekistan-in-uzbek-football-official-title-sponsor-of-the-pro-league.html)). Demak, Super League clip'lari uchun ham UzPFL, ham broadcaster ruxsati kerak bo'lishi mumkin. Bunday hamkorlik modeli dunyoda bor: LaLiga TikTok'ni rasmiy digital content partner qilib, creator'larni kontent strategiyasiga jalb qilgan ([Inside World Football](https://www.insideworldfootball.com/2024/09/23/laliga-gets-rolling-tiktok-content-partnership/)).

Yana ikkita toza yo'l bor. **EA Sports FC gameplay**: EA content policy YouTube va Twitch kabi partner programlar orqali monetize qilishga ruxsat beradi, lekin kontentni sotish yoki paywall ortiga qo'yishni taqiqlaydi ([EA Content Policy](https://help.ea.com/es/articles/security-and-rules/ea-content-policy/)). Faqat lokalizatsiya qilingan sahifalar tekshirilgan. **Taktik tahlil**: o'z grafikangiz, statistika va ovozingiz bilan, broadcast footage'siz qilingan tahlil Content ID'ga tushmaydi va "meaningful transformation" talabiga javob beradi. Uzbekistan'da YPP yo'qligi sababli hozirgi real daromad manbalari platformadan tashqarida: mahalliy brendlarning to'g'ridan-to'g'ri reklamasi, Telegram kanal reklamasi va affiliate havolalar.

## Xulosa

Texnik qism eng oson qism bo'lib chiqdi. "Qaysi model gol topadi" degan savolga eng yaxshi javob AI emas, scoreboard'dagi raqam o'zgarishi. Qimmat enterprise tizimlarni ham asosan footage huquqlari himoya qiladi, texnologiya emas. Shuning uchun bu vosita sotiladigan **mahsulot** sifatida qimmatroq, YouTube kanali uchun xom ashyo sifatida emas. Mahalliy klub, futbol maktabi, havaskor league va turnir tashkilotchilari o'z match'lari uchun avtomatik vertical highlight'ni qadrlaydi. Ular footage egasi, demak huquq muammosi yo'q. Siz esa Laravel tajribangiz bilan buni "match yukla → Shorts ol" degan SaaS'ga aylantira olasiz.

Tavsiya etilgan yo'l uch bosqichdan iborat. **Birinchi bosqich (1–2 hafta):** OpusClip Free yoki Klap'ning bepul videosini o'z yoki ruxsatli match faylida sinab ko'ring va generic vositalar qayerda xato qilishini o'zingiz ko'ring. **Ikkinchi bosqich (MVP):** RTX 5090'da FFmpeg/NVENC + PaddleOCR scoreboard + audio peak + faster-whisper asosida gol va xavfli moment'larni kesuvchi skriptni quring. Keyin RF-DETR va `supervision` bilan 9:16 crop hamda FFmpeg overlay'larini qo'shing. YOLO'dan va SoccerNet weights'dan voz keching, ixtiyoriy aniqlash uchun Gemini Flash-Lite'ni qo'shing (~$0.2 match boshiga). **Uchinchi bosqich (daromad):** mahalliy klublar, akademiyalar yoki UzPFL bilan footage huquqi yoki xizmat shartnomasi tuzing, Telegram va Instagram auditoriyasi va mahalliy sponsorlar orqali pul toping, YouTube YPP esa Uzbekistan uchun ochilguncha bonus bo'lib qolsin. Premier League, UCL yoki World Cup broadcast'larini kesib joylash uchun bu tizimni ishlatmang: Shorts bloklanadi va kanal strike'lar bilan yopiladi, bunga qo'shimcha ravishda bugun Uzbekistan'dan bu kontentdan YouTube orqali pul olishning yo'li ham yo'q.
